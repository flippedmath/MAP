Assessment Status Meanings

  • Active: Students can see this assessment in their view but cannot open it.
  • Open: Currently available for students to take assessment.
  • Upcoming: Scheduled to be set as 'Open' during specified time range.
  • Inactive: Hidden from students. Teachers can modify assessment.
  • Locked: Hidden from students. Teachers can not edit.
  • Retake Available: Allows eligible students to submit another attempt.
  • Closed: Open status has passed, no more student submissions accepted. Grading has come to a close.
  • Submitted: Phase for students submitting answers has passed, teacher has not graded yet.
